Stage 1 Step 7 Summary: FAST-LIO2 filter_size_surf comparison
=============================================================

Dataset: Parking_02
Baseline: filter_size_surf=0.5
Experiment: filter_size_surf=0.3

Main result:
Reducing filter_size_surf from 0.5 to 0.3 improved the trajectory position
metrics on this run. ATE position RMSE decreased from 0.303657 m to
0.148522 m. RPE translation RMSE decreased from 0.173436 m to 0.064888 m at
1 s and from 0.467672 m to 0.117748 m at 5 s.

Rotation:
Relative rotation RPE changed from 0.504116 deg to 0.509677 deg at 1 s and
from 1.853694 deg to 1.805768 deg at 5 s. The change is small compared with
the translation improvement.

Runtime and resources:
The experiment processed 627 frames with an average total processing time of
143.546 ms and an effective processing rate of 6.966 Hz. During bag playback,
average CPU was 36.632%, maximum CPU was 52.200%, and maximum RSS was
958664 KB. A separate default-group remeasurement processed 674 frames with
an average total processing time of 112.915 ms and an effective processing
rate of 8.856 Hz. Relative to that remeasurement, the experiment was 27.14%
slower per frame and had 21.34% lower effective processing rate. The runs
processed different frame counts, so this is a descriptive comparison rather
than an identical-frame benchmark.
